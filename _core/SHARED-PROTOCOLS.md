# Shared Protocols & Guardrails

This file serves as the single canonical registry for core operational protocols and guardrails. All agent workflows and system files must reference this document rather than duplicating these instructions.

---

## 1. Human-Sounding Writing Protocol

When producing user-facing or brand-facing copy:
1. **Analyze and Detect**: Check drafts for automated/robotic writing markers.
2. **Banned Vocabulary**: Do not use generic AI buzzwords (e.g., *revolutionize, foster, leverage, dive deep, dynamic, bespoke, realm, testament, elevate*).
3. **Sentence Structure**: Avoid uniform sentence lengths, excessive parallel structures, and predictable transitions.
4. **Action**: Rephrase to use direct, active verbs, conversational cadence, varied sentence structures, and concrete real-world details.

---

## 2. Interactive Experience Protocol

If requirements are underspecified or design options need alignment:
1. **Ask Strategically**: Do not proceed on critical assumptions.
2. **Be Structured**: Offer clear choices or structured options in the conversation when a decision materially changes the output.
3. **Format clearly**: Highlight recommended options first.
4. **Limit friction**: Focus questions on high-impact decisions that unblock the immediate milestone.

---

## 3. Source Integrity & Guardrails

- **Zero-Tolerance for Invention**: Never invent brand claims, market research data, font licenses, legal constraints, competitor stats, or performance metrics.
- **Handling Gaps**: Always flag missing information. Use `[To be supplied]` for missing factual inputs.
- **Draft Status**: External-facing copy remains "DRAFT" until verified source proof, terms, and compliance approvals are provided.

---

## 4. Missing Inputs Checklist

When a task is blocked by absent facts, flag the specific missing fields from this checklist:

### Brand
- Product/category, primary audience, core promise, desired perception.
- Competitors or references, first-use touchpoints.
- Mandatory assets, colors, fonts, claims, or legal rules.
- Rejected styles.

### Copy & Claims
- Proof points, source links/files, legal/compliance limits.
- Offer terms, voice examples, CTA, and destination.

### Visual & Design
- Approved/rejected visual references, logo/assets.
- Mandatory colors/fonts, usage rights/license source, output format/placement.

### Performance & Ads
- Platform, goal, budget/traffic level, conversion event.
- Tracking status, date range, current metrics, landing page.

### Lifecycle & Email
- Audience segment, consent/list status, platform.
- Trigger, timing, offer, success metric.
