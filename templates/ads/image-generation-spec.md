## Prompt Structure

```text
Create a static advertising image for [brand] targeting [audience].
Goal: [conversion or message job].
Scene: [subject, setting, action].
Composition: [layout, focal point, crop, negative space].
Style: [photography/illustration/3D/editorial/product render].
Brand cues: [colors, typography direction, logo placement if supplied].
Text overlay: [exact text or no text].
Format: [ratio, placement, safe area].
Avoid: [rejected styles, prohibited claims, visual cliches].
```

## QA

- Subject is clear in first glance.
- Text is legible and supplied by user or approved copy.
- No unsupported claims appear in the image.
- Brand assets and rights are supplied.
- Platform ratio and safe area are respected.
