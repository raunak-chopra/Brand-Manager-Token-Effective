# Content System

Use this skill for editorial strategy, content pillars, calendars, distribution, repurposing, and campaign content planning. It replaces `content-strategy`.

## Use For

- Content pillars and editorial direction.
- Campaign calendars and publishing plans.
- Funnel mapping across TOFU, MOFU, BOFU, and retention.
- Distribution and repurposing systems.
- Blog, newsletter, social, ad, and sales enablement content planning.

## Inputs

- Brand and audience.
- Business goal.
- Funnel stage.
- Channels.
- Available assets and resources.
- Cadence and timeline.
- Measurement needs.

## Workflow

1. Define content goal and audience intent.
2. Choose 3-5 pillars tied to problems, objections, proof, product, and culture.
3. Map content to funnel stages.
4. Choose formats and channels.
5. Build calendar with owner, date, format, CTA, asset need, and metric.
6. Add repurposing paths from large assets into smaller posts, emails, ads, or sales assets.
7. Define measurement and review cadence.

## Guardrails

- Do not create content volume without a distribution plan.
- Do not claim trends, benchmarks, or algorithm facts without sources.
- Keep brand-specific calendars and drafts inside `brands/[Brand]/`.
- Use channel-specific skills when execution depth is needed.

## Output

- Content pillars.
- Funnel map.
- Calendar or table.
- Distribution plan.
- Repurposing plan.
- Metrics.
- Missing assets and inputs.
