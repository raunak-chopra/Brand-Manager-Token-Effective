# Social Content System

Use this skill for organic social strategy and platform-native execution. It merges `social-media` and `social-content`.

## Use For

- Platform strategy, content pillars, cadence, engagement routines, and performance review.
- Posts, threads, carousels, short-form ideas, repurposing, and social calendars.
- Platform-native adaptation for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, and similar channels.

## Inputs

- Brand and audience.
- Platforms.
- Goal: awareness, engagement, traffic, trust, conversion, or community.
- Funnel stage.
- Current following or performance.
- Available content assets.
- Posting cadence and team constraints.

## Mode Routing

| Mode | Use When | Load References |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Platform choice, cadence, growth, engagement, or review loop. | `social-media-playbook.md`. |
| Posts | Platform-ready posts, hooks, threads, captions, or carousels. | `post-templates.md`, `platform-limits.md`. |
| Formats | The user needs format selection or content types. | `content-formats.md`, `platforms.md`. |

| Algorithms | Only when current platform mechanics are explicitly needed. | `platform-algorithms.md`; verify externally if recency matters. |

## Workflow

1. Choose platform and content job.
2. Pick content pillar and funnel role.
3. Write hook, body, CTA, and format notes.
4. Adapt to platform norms and limits.
5. Add repurposing and scheduling notes.
6. Define engagement and review plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not chase virality at the cost of brand fit.
- Avoid one-size-fits-all cross-posting.
- Do not invent algorithm facts, trends, social proof, stats, or claims.
- Do not use hashtags by default; use only when platform/context supports them.

## Output

- Platform strategy or platform-ready posts.
- Content pillars.
- Hook alternatives.
- Visual or carousel notes.
- Calendar table if requested.
- Repurposing ideas.
- Metrics to watch.
