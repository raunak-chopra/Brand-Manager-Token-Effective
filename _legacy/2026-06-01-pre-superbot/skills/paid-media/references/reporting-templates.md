## 2. Creative Scorecard Builder

**Purpose**: Composite scoring system to rank active ads and identify which to scale, maintain, or replace.

### Scoring Formula

| Component | Weight | Metric | How to Score |
|-----------|--------|--------|-------------|
| CTR | 25% | Click-through rate | (Ad CTR ÷ Campaign average CTR) × 25. Cap at 25. |
| Conversion Rate | 25% | CVR (conversions ÷ clicks) | (Ad CVR ÷ Campaign average CVR) × 25. Cap at 25. |
| ROAS | 20% | Return on ad spend | (Ad ROAS ÷ Target ROAS) × 20. Cap at 20. |
| Cost Efficiency | 15% | CPA vs. target CPA | (Target CPA ÷ Ad CPA) × 15. Cap at 15. If Ad CPA > Target, ratio < 1. |
| Longevity | 15% | Days active with stable performance | (Days active ÷ 90) × 15. Cap at 15. |

**Total Score** = Sum of all component scores. Maximum = 100.

### Classification

| Score Range | Classification | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| 80–100 | Star Performer | Scale budget. Use as template for new creative. Protect from unnecessary changes. |
| 60–79 | Solid | Maintain. Monitor for fatigue. Keep in rotation. |
| 40–59 | Underperforming | Diagnose: is it creative, audience, or placement? Test variants. Set 2-week improvement deadline. |
| Below 40 | Replace Immediately | Pause ad. Launch replacement. Analyze why it failed to inform future creative. |

## Guardrails

- All data fields use `[To be supplied]` for brand-specific values.
- Do not invent performance data, benchmarks, or competitive facts.
- State attribution model and window before presenting ROAS figures.
- Note data freshness and reporting lag where applicable (Meta: up to 72 hours; Google: up to 24 hours).
- Distinguish between platform-reported conversions and verified conversions (CRM, backend) when available.
- For competitor analysis, use only publicly available data from Meta Ad Library. Do not fabricate competitor metrics.
