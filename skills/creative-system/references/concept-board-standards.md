# Concept Board Standards

Use a concept board when an idea needs to be reviewed, approved, or handed to production.

## Board Structure

- Context.
- Selected route.
- Strategic insight.
- Audience tension.
- Campaign platform.
- Key message.
- Visual direction.
- Copy samples.
- Image prompts or generated concept slots.
- Channel mockups or placement notes.
- Decision scorecard.
- Missing inputs.
- Next production steps.

## Stakeholder Review Checklist

| Check | Pass Condition |
|---|---|
| 3-second memory | The idea is understandable and memorable quickly. |
| Ownable world | Visuals, language, or format feel specific to the brand. |
| Audience tension | The concept addresses a real audience pressure, desire, or contradiction. |
| Brand role | The brand has a clear reason to own the idea. |
| Channel fit | The concept can work in the intended placement. |
| Scale | The idea can become at least 5 assets or touchpoints. |
| Claim safety | Unsupported claims, proof, and legal risks are marked. |
| Asset readiness | References, fonts, images, and rights are supplied or marked `[To be supplied]`. |

## Recommendation Rule

Recommend the route with the best mix of brand fit, distinctiveness, memorability, production feasibility, and source safety.
