# Big Idea Frameworks

A big idea is a memorable campaign platform that can become many assets. It should compress audience tension, brand role, and visual world into a phrase or device people can recognize quickly.

## Frameworks

- Tension inversion: flip the audience's assumed problem.
- Enemy creation: name the force the brand helps people fight.
- Category reframing: change what the buyer thinks the category is really about.
- Ritual creation: turn use into a repeatable behavior or moment.
- Status signal: make adoption feel like belonging to a sharper group.
- Before/after world: dramatize a change without inventing outcomes.
- Object metaphor: make an abstract promise visible through one object or scene.
- Social proof theatre: show proof as a scene, not an unsupported claim.
- Founder myth: turn conviction and origin into a repeatable creative device.
- Product mechanism dramatization: make the product's method visible and memorable.

## Required Fields

For each idea, include:

- Idea name.
- One-line campaign platform.
- Why people care.
- What makes it ownable.
- What it looks like.
- What it sounds like.
- How it becomes multiple assets.
- Proof needed.
- Claim or asset risks.

## Development Pattern

1. Generate 10 rough seeds.
2. Cluster them into 3-5 platforms.
3. Expand each platform into hero execution, copy examples, image direction, channels, and production notes.
4. Score before recommending.
