# Creative Quality Rubric

Score each criterion from 1-5.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distinctiveness | Generic category idea | Some recognizable angle | Ownable, sharp, hard to confuse |
| Audience truth | Vague audience benefit | Plausible tension | Specific lived tension or desire |
| Brand fit | Could belong to anyone | Fits with edits | Feels native to this brand |
| Emotional pull | Informational only | Useful but mild | Creates curiosity, desire, pride, relief, or urgency |
| Visual memorability | Flat or stock-like | Clear but familiar | Strong image, scene, object, or format |
| Copy strength | Generic phrasing | Clear message | Memorable language with a point of view |
| Channel usefulness | Hard to place | Works in one channel | Scales across multiple assets/channels |
| Production feasibility | Needs unclear assets | Possible with effort | Can be produced with known resources |
| Claim safety | Depends on unsupported claims | Some proof gaps | Safe or clearly marked with `[To be supplied]` |
| Asset readiness | Rights unclear | Some gaps | Assets supplied, approved, or clearly logged |

## Recommendation Rule

Recommend only routes scoring 4+ on brand fit, distinctiveness, and production feasibility.

Do not recommend a route with unresolved high claim, legal, privacy, or asset-rights risk. Mark it as promising but blocked instead.
