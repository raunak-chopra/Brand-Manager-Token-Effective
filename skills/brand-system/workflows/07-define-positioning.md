# Define Positioning

Use to create or refine category, target, differentiator, reason to believe, and positioning statement.

## Inputs

Audience research, competitor audit, product promise, proof, category frame, offer, and constraints.

## Process

1. Load brand context and relevant research notes.
2. Use `references/brand-positioning-theory.md` for positioning structure.
3. Define target, category, problem, differentiated promise, proof, enemy/alternative, and tradeoffs.
4. Flag unsupported proof, market claims, or competitor claims as `[To be supplied]`.
5. Save the positioning system in `brands/[Brand]/brand-book.md` or a shareable brief in `outputs/[Brand]/`.

## Output

Positioning statement, message hierarchy implications, proof gaps, and recommended next workflow.
