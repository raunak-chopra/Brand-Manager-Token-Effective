# Define Voice

Use to define brand voice, tone range, vocabulary, and writing constraints.

## Inputs

Positioning, archetype, audience language, existing copy samples, banned terms, channel needs, and claim limits.

## Process

1. Load brand context and any supplied copy samples.
2. Use `references/brand-voice-development.md` for voice structure when needed.
3. Define voice traits, tone sliders, vocabulary, banned patterns, example rewrites, and channel adaptations.
4. Keep external-facing claims in draft status until proof and compliance limits are supplied.
5. Save durable voice rules in `brands/[Brand]/brand-book.md` and concise rules in `context-index.md`.

## Output

Voice guide with examples, channel adaptations, banned patterns, and missing proof/approval notes.
