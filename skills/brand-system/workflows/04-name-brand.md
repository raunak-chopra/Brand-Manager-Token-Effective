# Name Brand

Use to generate, evaluate, or narrow brand names.

## Inputs

Category, audience, promise, positioning, tone, rejected words/styles, legal/domain constraints, and naming preferences.

## Process

1. Load brand context and relevant positioning/voice inputs.
2. Ask for hard constraints before ideation: language, length, domain preference, trademark sensitivity, and banned terms.
3. Generate options by naming route: descriptive, evocative, compound, metaphor, founder-led, or coined.
4. Evaluate options for clarity, memorability, category fit, voice fit, conflict risk, and claim risk.
5. Treat domain, trademark, and legal availability as `[To be supplied]` unless verified from supplied sources or explicit research.

## Output

Shortlisted names with rationale, risks, open checks, and a recommended next step.
