# Select Archetype

Use to choose a brand archetype or archetype blend that guides tone, visuals, and customer experience.

## Inputs

Audience motivation, desired perception, positioning, founder style, voice constraints, and rejected personality traits.

## Process

1. Load brand context and positioning.
2. Use `references/brand-archetype-selection.md` when deeper archetype analysis is needed.
3. Compare archetypes by customer motivation, brand promise, category fit, and risk of cliche.
4. Define primary archetype, secondary influence, behaviors, voice implications, and visual implications.
5. Save the chosen archetype in `brands/[Brand]/brand-book.md`.

## Output

Archetype choice, rationale, do/don't behaviors, and implications for voice and visual direction.
