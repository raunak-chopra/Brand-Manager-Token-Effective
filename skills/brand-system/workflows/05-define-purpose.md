# Define Purpose

Use to turn founder and audience inputs into purpose, mission, vision, and belief statements.

## Inputs

Founder brief, customer problem, category point of view, audience outcome, proof, values, and business constraints.

## Process

1. Load brand context and the founder/audience notes.
2. Use `references/golden-circle-purpose.md` when the purpose needs deeper structure.
3. Draft purpose, mission, vision, belief, and strategic tension.
4. Test each statement for specificity, truthfulness, and operational usefulness.
5. Save durable strategy in `brands/[Brand]/brand-book.md` or `brands/[Brand]/context-index.md`.

## Output

Purpose system with draft status, evidence notes, and `[To be supplied]` gaps.
