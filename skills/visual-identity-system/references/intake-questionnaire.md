# Visual Identity Intake Questionnaire

Ask only what is missing. Do not force the user through every question if existing context answers it.

## Essential Questions

1. What is the product or brand?
2. What category does it compete in?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. Who is the primary audience?
5. What should people feel within 3 seconds of seeing the brand?
6. What is the single most important brand promise?
7. Which competitors or category references should we understand?
8. Which brands should we not look or sound like?
9. Where will the identity be used first?
10. What reference folders should be used?

## Strategic Questions

| Area | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | What does the audience already believe about this category? | Reveals trust gaps and visual expectations |
| Perception | Should the brand feel expert, warm, premium, disruptive, clinical, playful, or something else? | Sets the visual tone |
| Category | What visual codes are common in the category? | Helps decide what to keep or break |
| Differentiation | What should make this brand feel unlike competitors? | Prevents generic identity work |
| Trust | What proof points must the design support? | Guides claims, seals, data, and layout hierarchy |
| Channel | Is the first use case packaging, web, social, ads, retail, or app? | Changes design priorities |
| Constraints | Any mandatory colors, fonts, logos, claims, legal rules, or print limitations? | Avoids unusable recommendations |
| Rejection | What styles are explicitly wrong for the brand? | Reduces wasted exploration |
