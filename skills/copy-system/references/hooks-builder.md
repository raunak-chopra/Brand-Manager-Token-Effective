# Marketing Hooks & Copywriting Guides

A brief guide on copy hooks, scroll-stoppers, and psychological triggers for ads, social posts, and landers.

## Hook Types & Scroll-Stoppers

- **The Curiosity Gap**: State a surprising fact or half-told truth that forces the reader to continue to find out the rest. (e.g., *"Why 90% of brand builders fail in the first month..."*)
- **The Direct Problem**: Address a specific, painful problem the audience is experiencing immediately. (e.g., *"Struggling with high CPC and low CTR? Try this..."*)
- **The Contrast/Paradox**: Present two conflicting ideas that challenge common assumptions. (e.g., *"How spending less on ads got us 2x more conversions..."*)
- **The Social Proof**: Lead with a customer quote, review, or case-study result. (e.g., *"1,000+ brand owners switched to our template last week. Here's why..."*)

## Execution Guidelines

1. **Keep it under 3 seconds to read**: The first line must be ultra-punchy and visible before any "read more" truncations.
2. **Action-oriented**: Every hook must transition smoothly into a core promise and a clear call-to-action (CTA).
3. **Tone check**: Ensure hooks follow the brand voice guidelines from the active brand context index.
