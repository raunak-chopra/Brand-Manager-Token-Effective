# Deep Research

Use when a decision needs broader synthesis across sources, competitors, audience language, trends, or evidence quality.

## Inputs

Research objective, target market, audience, known sources, required freshness, decision criteria, and output format.

## Process

1. Define the research question and decision the answer must support.
2. Load local brand context and supplied sources before external research.
3. Browse current or high-stakes facts when needed and cite sources.
4. Synthesize by themes: confirmed facts, contradictions, opportunities, risks, and missing evidence.
5. Save durable findings in `brands/[Brand]/reference-library/` and shareable reports in `outputs/[Brand]/`.

## Output

Evidence-backed research brief with source notes, confidence levels, open questions, and recommended next steps.
