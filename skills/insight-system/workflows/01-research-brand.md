# Research Brand

Use for focused brand, audience, competitor, or category research.

## Inputs

Research question, brand/category, audience, supplied sources, competitors, market, geography, and decision the research should support.

## Process

1. Load brand context first when a brand is named.
2. Search or browse only when current external facts are required.
3. Separate confirmed source evidence, reasonable inference, and `[To be supplied]` gaps.
4. Capture useful source links or local source paths.
5. Save durable research in `brands/[Brand]/reference-library/` or shareable summaries in `outputs/[Brand]/`.

## Output

Research summary with evidence, implications, risks, and recommended next action.
