# Insight System

Use for customer research, personas, VOC, JTBD, competitor synthesis, source-backed category notes, and intake discovery.

## Inputs

Research question, brand/category, audience hypothesis, available sources, competitor/reference set, decision to support.

## Modes

| Mode | Use When | Reference Guide | Action Workflow Playbook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intake** | starting brand discover or auditing a founder | `../brand-system/references/golden-circle-purpose.md` | `../brand-system/workflows/01-discover-founder.md` |
| **Audience** | profiling target ICP, VOC, and customer psychographics | `references/jtbd-psychographic-research.md` | `../brand-system/workflows/02-research-audience.md` |
| **Competitor** | conducting competitive brand and visual audits | `../visual-system/references/competitive-visual-audit.md` | `workflows/01-research-brand.md`<br>`../brand-system/workflows/03-audit-competitors.md` |
| **Deep Research** | running structured market or brand analysis | `references/jtbd-psychographic-research.md` | `workflows/02-deep-research.md` |

## Workflow

1. State the research question and context.
2. Identify the active research **Mode**.
3. Load the corresponding **Reference Guide** and **Action Workflow Playbook**.
4. Conduct structured interviews or market searches following the playbook.
5. Translate raw insights into brand, copy, and campaign recommendations.
6. Group findings into confirmed facts vs recommendations, noting evidence gaps.

## Output

ICP profiles, JTBD timeline summaries, competitive landscape grids, VOC reports, and next-action research playbooks.
