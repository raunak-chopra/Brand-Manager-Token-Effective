# Scale Business

Use for growth roadmaps, paid media optimization, reporting, and experiment planning.

## Inputs

Goal, platform, budget/traffic, conversion event, tracking status, current metrics, offer, landing page, creative, audience, date range, and hygiene findings source.

## Process

1. Load brand context and confirm performance mode: optimization, growth plan, reporting, or weekly memo.
2. Apply the Hygiene Gate from `skills/performance-system/SKILL.md`.
3. If readiness is `Blocked`, output hygiene fixes instead of performance conclusions.
4. If readiness is `Qualified`, analyze metrics with visible caveats.
5. If readiness is `Interpretable`, diagnose offer, audience, creative, landing page, tracking, and budget mechanics.
6. Rank actions by expected impact, confidence, effort, owner, and required approval.
7. Save reports in `outputs/[Brand]/`; save durable learnings in `brands/[Brand]/experiments-backlog.md` or `brands/[Brand]/learnings-registry.md`.

## Output

Prioritized growth plan, performance memo, or experiment backlog with source-data caveats and approval requirements.
