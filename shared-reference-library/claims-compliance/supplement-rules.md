# Nutraceutical Claims Compliance Guidelines (Supplement Rules)

Use this guide to validate copywriting, advertisements, packaging copy, and website claims for supplement brands.

---

## 1. Core Regulatory Constraints (India - FSSAI)

Supplement brands targeting the Indian market must adhere to the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulations for health supplements, nutraceuticals, and foods for special dietary uses.

### Forbidden Claims
* **No Disease Prevention/Cure:** Do not state or imply that the supplement cures, mitigates, treats, or prevents any specific disease, disorder, or medical condition.
* **No Exaggerated Timelines:** Avoid saying "heals in 3 days", "cures sleep issues overnight", or similar specific speed guarantees unless clinically validated.
* **No Medical Wording:** Avoid words like "prescribed," "medicine," "drug," "treatment," "patient," "dosage" (use "serving size"), or "miracle pill."

### Permitted Claims
* **Structure-Function Claims:** Describe how a nutrient/ingredient supports the normal structure or function of the body (e.g., "helps support healthy digestion," "supports normal cellular function").
* **Absorption / Bioavailability Claims:** Permitted only when backed by ingredient science, clinical validation, or another supplied source.
* **Nutritional Content Claims:** Stating the presence of vitamins or minerals (e.g., "high in Vitamin D3").

---

## 2. Mandatory Disclaimers

Every supplement creative piece (landing page, packaging, ad copy body) must contain or have access to the standard disclaimer:

> **"These statements have not been evaluated by the FSSAI. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."**

---

## 3. Claim Audit Matrix

Use this matrix to audit all newly generated ad copy or copy draft variants:

| Wording Type | Examples (High Risk) | Compliant Alternative | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cure Claims** | "Cures Vitamin B12 deficiency." | "Helps maintain healthy Vitamin B12 levels." | Supplements support health, they do not cure deficiencies. |
| **Disease Claims** | "Prevents arthritis and joint pain." | "Supports joint mobility and flexibility." | Avoid promising disease prevention. |
| **Exaggeration** | "Instantly absorbs 100% of nutrients." | "Formulated for enhanced absorption and bioavailability." | Be scientifically accurate and avoid absolute superlatives. |
| **Weight Loss** | "Melts fat and cures obesity." | "Supports healthy weight management when combined with a balanced diet." | Weight claims are highly scrutinized. |

---

## 4. Bot Compliance Workflow

1. **Scan Draft Copy:** Bot scans the text for any health, ingredient, or performance claims.
2. **Flag Violations:** If any words from the *Forbidden* list or *High Risk* examples are found, flag them immediately.
3. **Draft Alternative:** Replace with compliant structure-function alternatives.
4. **Insert Disclaimers:** Ensure the FSSAI/standard disclaimer is appended to the footer of the copy set.
