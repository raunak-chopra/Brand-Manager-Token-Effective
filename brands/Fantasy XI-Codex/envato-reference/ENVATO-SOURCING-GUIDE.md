## Customization Rules

- Replace template colors with:
  - Deep Navy: `#07111F`
  - Electric Green: `#12B76A`
  - Chalk White: `#F7F7F2`
  - Signal Amber: `#F5B841`
  - Risk Red: `#EF4444`
- Replace generic text with `Deadline XI`, `Papa Ranjeet`, `LOCK`, `WATCH`, `PUNT`, `AVOID`.
- Avoid official FIFA logos, trophy graphics, and player likenesses unless usage rights are confirmed.
