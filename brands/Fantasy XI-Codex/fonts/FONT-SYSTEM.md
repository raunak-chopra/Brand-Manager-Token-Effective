## Recommended Pairing

Primary display:
- `WORLD Condensed` or `Bigstage`

Body/UI:
- `Crawford`, `Manuver Rounded`, or system sans

Numbers/Data:
- `Inter Soccer`, `Roboto Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`, or system monospace

## Usage

- Channel logo: condensed display, uppercase
- Thumbnails: condensed display, 3-5 word max
- Lower thirds: display for player name, body sans for details
- Stat cards: mono for price, ownership, fixture, and risk tags
