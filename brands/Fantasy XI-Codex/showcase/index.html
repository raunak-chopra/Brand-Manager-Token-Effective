Brand Position

Fantasy football is noisy: deadlines, squads, rotation, fixtures, captains, prices, and last-minute panic. Deadline XI owns the simple moment viewers care about: what should I do before lock?

Core Promise

Fast, practical fantasy football calls that tell the viewer what to lock, what to watch, what to punt, and what to avoid before deadline.

Audience

Indian and global football fans who play fantasy but do not have time to track every press conference, lineup leak, injury update, and fixture swing.

Owned Word

Deadline-ready. The channel becomes associated with the last useful decision window before lock.

Onliness Statement

Deadline XI is the fantasy football content brand that turns football chaos into simple Hinglish XI decisions for time-poor fantasy managers.

Use Deadline XI as the channel name and Papa Ranjeet as the recurring host/persona. That gives the system a clean evergreen brand and a memorable human voice.

Content System

The content is built around repeatable decisions. Every format should help the viewer act faster: captain, transfer, draft fix, player watch, or avoid.

Channel Roles

Each channel has a different job, but all of them point back to one behavior: watch before lock, comment your draft, and return for the next decision.

Faceless Workflow

Screen recording of fantasy dashboard, tactical board overlays, voiceover, avatar corner, quick zooms, and decision chips. Best for Shorts, Reels, and daily updates.

Face Version

Talking-head Papa Ranjeet with dark football backdrop, stat-card overlays, and direct eye-contact calls. Best for trust, long-form, and draft review episodes.
