# Fantasy XI Context Index

## Brand Snapshot

- Working name: Fantasy XI
- Category: Fantasy football content channel for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and YouTube long-form
- Presenter Persona: Papa Ranjeet (The Lab Manager / Bada Bhai)
- Language Style: Hinglish (Hindi/English conversational mix)
- First campaign: FIFA World Cup 2026 Fantasy launch
- Start date: 2026-06-10 India time
- Tournament opener: 2026-06-11, Mexico vs South Africa, Mexico City Stadium
- Core audience: Indian and global football fans who want practical fantasy picks without watching every press conference
- Primary promise: Fast, fixture-aware fantasy football decisions before deadline
- Desired perception: Sharp, calm, useful, early, and accountable
- Claim status: Draft. Player picks, injury status, odds, prices, and rules must be verified before publishing.

## Positioning

Fantasy XI is the fantasy football channel for managers who want deadline-ready picks, captain calls, and differential ideas in a short, repeatable format.

- **Onliness statement:** Fantasy XI is the only fantasy football content lab that turns World Cup chaos into simple daily XI decisions for time-poor managers.
- **Owned word:** Deadline-ready.

## Persona & Presentation Workflows

- **Name:** Papa Ranjeet
- **Character:** A smart, stats-savvy elder brother ("bada bhai") who cuts through statistics and gives clean recommendations with light-hearted humor.
- **Tone:** Hinglish (conversational, colloquial mixture of Hindi and English).
- **Signature phrases:**
  - "Tension-free pick hai, lock kar do."
  - "Naam bada, par minutes ka kya?"
  - "Risk hai toh ishq hai, let's punt."
  - "Aankh band karke captain mat banao."
  - "Maza aega!"
- **Production Setups:**
  - **Face Setup:** Papa Ranjeet on camera (talking head) with a clean dark backdrop or green screen displaying player stats cards. High-energy, hand gestures, premium mic audio.
  - **Faceless Setup:** Screenshare of the World Cup Fantasy dashboard or tactical board (deep navy/pitch green) with a dynamic animated avatar of Papa Ranjeet in the corner. Quick zooms and mouse highlights.

## Claims And Proof

- **Approved Claims:** Deadlines, player prices, and rules verified against official FIFA app
- **Proof Sources:** Official FIFA World Cup Fantasy 2026 platform
- **Legal Limits:** No betting or gambling promotions. "Not financial advice" disclaimer for player picks.
- **Font/Asset Licenses:** Envato Elements licensed fonts and video assets (to be sourced by user)

## Channel Roles

- **Instagram Reels/Stories:** Fast discovery, high engagement, story polls for captaincy choices, and DM team reviews.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Search-friendly differential picks and quick 45-second team building tricks.
- **YouTube Long-form:** Deeper trust through 6–8 minute tactical previews, rolling captaincy route planning, and full draft reviews.

## Content Pillars

1. **Deadline Calls:** Captains, transfers, team news, last checks
2. **Fixture Lab:** Fixture swings, clean sheet routes, attacking matchups
3. **Player Lab:** Safe picks, differentials, budget enablers, avoid traps
4. **Draft Doctor:** Review viewer teams and fix weak spots
5. **Results Review:** What worked, what failed, what changes tomorrow

## Visual Direction

- **Style:** tactical board plus neon broadcast graphics
- **Palette:** deep navy (`#07111F`), pitch green (`#12B76A`), off-white (`#F7F7F2`), amber accent (`#F5B841`), red risk marker (`#EF4444`)
- **Typography direction:** condensed sport headline plus clean sans body
- **Avoid:** gambling aesthetics, fake betting claims, overused trophy clipart, cluttered thumbnails
