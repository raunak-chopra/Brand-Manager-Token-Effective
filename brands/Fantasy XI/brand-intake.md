## Phase 1: Discovery

### Brand / Product
**Fantasy XI** is an entertainment and advisory brand helping players build winning fantasy football squads for the 2026 FIFA World Cup Fantasy and beyond.

### Category
Digital Content Creation (Sports Entertainment & Data Analytics).

### Primary Audience
*   Core: Indian and global football fans playing World Cup Fantasy.
*   Profile: Time-poor, competitive, plays in active office/friends leagues, wants simple transfer and captaincy guidance.

### Core Promise
Deadline-ready fantasy calls without the noise. "Tension-free advice".

### Desired Perception
*   Practical, analytical, sharp, accountable, and highly entertaining (Bada Bhai vibe).

## Phase 3: Messaging

### Elevator Pitch
"World Cup Fantasy is chaotic, but your team building doesn't have to be. Papa Ranjeet cuts through the press conference noise to give you the exact daily Lock, Watch, Punt, and Avoid picks you need before lock. Simple Hinglish advice for smart fantasy managers."

### Value Pillars
1.  **Time Efficiency:** Clear decisions in under 45 seconds.
2.  **Minutes Over Names:** Focused on guaranteed starting minutes, not just famous reputations.
3.  **Hinglish Colloquial Tone:** Engaging and easy to understand (conversational and protective, like a big brother).
4.  **Calculated Punts:** Structured differential recommendations ("Risk hai toh ishq hai").
