## 2. Daily Production Routine

To maintain consistency, follow this daily rhythm based on your selected video format:

### A. Morning (9:00 AM - 11:30 AM) - Scan & Script
1.  **News Scan:** Check Twitter and squad news for injuries, press conference summaries, and verified starting lineups.
2.  **Scripting:** Write the daily short-form script in Hinglish based on the day's objective (Lock, Punt, Avoid).

### B. Afternoon (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM) - Record & Edit
*   **Face Setup:** Set up your camera/phone. Position yourself with the **Broadcasting Backdrop** displayed on your green screen/TV. Record Papa Ranjeet's segments. Cut files and add player stats graphics using bold fonts.
*   **Faceless Setup:** Open the World Cup Fantasy dashboard. Record screen segments showing players, prices, and match tables. Sync with your Hinglish voiceover. Overlay Papa Ranjeet's caricature avatar in the corner.

### C. Late Afternoon (4:00 PM - 5:30 PM) - Publish & Engage
1.  Upload Reels/Shorts with target hashtags: `#WorldCupFantasy`, `#FantasyFootball`, `#PapaRanjeet`.
2.  Post Instagram interactive stories (polls or question boxes for draft submissions).
