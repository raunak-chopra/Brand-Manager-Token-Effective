## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand Summary
**Fantasy XI** (formerly Fantasy XI / Fantasy XI) is a fast-paced, high-impact fantasy football content channel. It translates the massive noise of injury reports, statistics, and press conferences into simple, daily deadline-ready decisions.

### Presenter Persona (Papa Ranjeet)
*   **60% Bada Bhai (Big Brother Analyst):** Friendly, protects the viewer's fantasy points, uses terms like *"Tension-free points"* and *"Bhaiya"*.
*   **30% Explorer:** Hunts for differentials and early fixture swings.
*   **10% Showman:** Witty, confident, uses catchphrases like *"Risk hai toh ishq hai"*, *"Maza aega!"*.

### Positioning Statement
For fantasy football managers in India and globally who don't have hours to read transcripts and watch press conferences, **Fantasy XI** is a social content lab that turns chaotic matches into daily XI decisions. Unlike noisy, hype-filled channels, we present clear labels (**Lock**, **Watch**, **Punt**, **Avoid**) with a friendly, big-brother analyst voice.

## 2. Visual Strategy

### Visual Idea
A high-tech digital analyst lab combined with dynamic broadcast graphics. The aesthetic uses dark layouts with glowing neon green tactical paths, data highlights, and bold sport typography.

### Design Principles
1.  **Lead with the Decision:** Put player name, price, and status labels prominently. Don't hide the answer behind stats.
2.  **Sleek Dark Mode:** Always use a dark backdrop so the neon green and signal amber colors immediately pop out.
3.  **Sports-Broadcast High-BPM Energy:** Fast-paced cuts, sliding visual graphics, and clear indicators.

### Category Codes To Keep
*   Condensed bold numbers for prices and scores.
*   Green arrows for transfers/rises; red arrows for drops/injuries.
*   Dark backgrounds with stadium turf green accents.

### Category Codes To Break
*   No cheesy AI cartoon player face swaps.
*   No clickbait circles and arrows pointing to random player faces.
*   No flashy betting/casino layouts or dollar icons.

## 4. Typography (Envato Soccer Assets Rework)

### Primary & Secondary Fonts
*   **Primary Display Headline:** **World Modern Soccer Cup Font** (`envato-60ab` / local path: `fonts/vendor-envato/world/WORLD-Condensed.ttf`). Highly condensed, modern, bold soccer display font. Use for all player name overlays, large titles, and main thumbnail headers.
*   **Jersey & Scorebug Numbers:** **Inter-Soccer Jersey Outline Font** (`envato-3c13` / local path: `fonts/vendor-envato/inter-soccer/Inter-Soccer.ttf`). Outline sports display font. Use for jersey numbers, match results, and scorebug assets.
*   **Social Accent & Captions:** **Nutmeg League Font** (`envato-2012`) or **Manuver Rounded** (`envato-20260604-00201`). Rounded, bold social font. Use for quick text callouts on stories and highlighted player status tags.
*   **Technical Stats:** **IBM Plex Mono** (Google Fonts). Clean monospaced font. Use for player prices (Â£5.5m), percentages (4.2% ownership), and stats grids (xG, xA).

## 5. Visual Asset Guidelines

### Social Media Thumbnails
*   **Max 3-5 Words:** A bold, short question (e.g. "GW1 CAPTAIN CALL?").
*   **One Player Cutout:** High-quality PNG photo of the player with a soft green outline glow.
*   **Status Chip:** One clear chip indicating **Lock**, **Punt**, or **Avoid** in its corresponding color.

### Video Overlays (Face & Faceless)
*   **Lower Thirds:** Dark translucent card with a thick Pitch Green left border containing name and price.
*   **Transition Wipe:** High-speed motion blur swipe using the brand colors.
*   **Data Modules:** Grid cells showing player statistics in mono font.
