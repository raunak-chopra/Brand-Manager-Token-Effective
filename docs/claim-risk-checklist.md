# Claim Risk Checklist

Use this for external-facing copy, ads, emails, landing pages, brand books, social posts, and performance reports.

## Claim Types

| Claim Type | Examples | Required Support |
|---|---|---|
| Performance | faster, better, higher ROAS, improves conversion | Data source, timeframe, method, caveat. |
| Health or clinical | clinically proven, absorption, safe, effective | Approved source document and legal review. |
| Comparative | best, leading, better than competitor | Competitor source, date, method, jurisdiction. |
| Customer proof | testimonials, reviews, case studies | Permission, exact quote, source path. |
| Guarantee | risk-free, guaranteed, results promised | Approved legal wording and terms. |
| Pricing or offer | discount, limited time, free, savings | Offer rules, dates, eligibility, exclusions. |
| Technical or proprietary | patented, AI-powered, unique technology | Patent/source proof or technical documentation. |
| Sustainability or ethics | eco-friendly, clean, ethical, fair | Certification, standard, or approved wording. |

## Review Steps

1. Identify every explicit and implied claim.
2. Classify claim type.
3. Check source path and approved wording.
4. Replace unsupported facts with `[To be supplied]`.
5. Remove exaggeration, universal promises, and vague superiority.
6. Flag legal review when category, market, or wording requires it.

## Output Format

| Copy/Claim | Risk | Support Status | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[claim]` | Low/Medium/High | Supplied / `[To be supplied]` | Keep, revise, or remove. |

## Guardrails

- Do not invent source references, citations, reviews, metrics, or legal rules.
- Do not soften unsupported claims into new unsupported claims.
- Do not use claim-heavy wording when the proof path is unknown.
- For regulated categories, use only approved wording.
